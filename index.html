About the shop

A proper barber shop,
done the right way.

Sam's Barbers sits on Vittoria Street, in the middle of Birmingham's historic Jewellery Quarter. No gimmicks, no rushed jobs — just careful, consistent barbering that keeps a perfect 5.0 rating across more than a hundred reviews.

Every cut starts with a proper consultation and ends with a clean finish. Book ahead on Booksy and your chair is waiting for you — walk-ins, kids and card payments are all welcome too.
